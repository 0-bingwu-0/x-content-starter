# Saving Is Not the Hard Part: What 10 User Interviews Taught Me About Notes

Last week I talked to 10 people who use note apps every day. I asked them one simple question: "When was the last time you found something useful in your notes?"

Most of them paused. A few laughed. One person said, "Does finding a recipe count?"

I'm building a personal knowledge base, so this was a bit uncomfortable to hear. But it also made one thing very clear.

## Everyone saves. Almost no one goes back.

All 10 people had a system for saving things. Bookmarks, read-later apps, screenshots, voice memos, notes in three different apps.

Saving felt good. It felt like progress.

But when I asked how they used those notes later, the answers got vague. Most of them only went back when they were looking for one specific thing, like an address or a password hint. The ideas, articles and meeting notes they saved "for later" mostly stayed there.

## Why finding is harder than saving

From the interviews, I saw three reasons:

* **You don't remember what you saved.** You can't search for something you forgot exists.
* **You don't remember the words.** You saved an article about "pricing," but now you're searching for "how much to charge."
* **The note is in the wrong place.** It's in a different app from the one you're working in.

None of these are about saving. They are all about the moment you actually need the information.

## What AI changes

This is where I think AI really helps, and where it doesn't.

AI is good at the second problem. It can search by meaning, so "how much to charge" can find your note about pricing.

AI can also help with the first problem. When you're writing about a topic, it can bring up related notes you forgot about.

But AI can't help if your notes aren't there. ChatGPT knows a lot about the world. It doesn't know what you read last month, what your customers said, or what you decided in a meeting. That context is yours.

So I don't think AI makes notes less important. I think it makes them more useful, as long as AI can actually read them.

## What I'm building next

Based on these interviews, I'm changing what I work on first. Less time on making saving faster. More time on bringing the right note back while you're working.

I'll share how it goes. And if you have a system that actually works for finding old notes, I'd really like to hear about it.
