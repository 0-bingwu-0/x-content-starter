# My Profile

Read this before suggesting topics or writing posts. Below is an example setup; replace it with your own.

## Background

I'm a builder and founder working on a personal knowledge base product. It helps people keep their notes, saved material, and ideas in one place, so they can find them and use them when they need them.

## Posting goals

* **Build my personal brand:** Let people who care about AI and knowledge management know who I am and what I'm building, and trust my judgment.
* **Grow users for the product:** Reach heavy users of notes and knowledge bases, and get them interested in what I'm building.
* **Meet peers:** Swap product thinking with other builders and founders.

## Posting angle

I look at each day's AI news as a founder building a personal knowledge base:

* How will this change the way people save, find, and use what they know?
* Is it an opportunity or a threat for my product, and how would I respond?
* Do I have a related experience, user feedback, or call from building the product?

Ideas I keep coming back to:

* Saving things is easy. The hard part is finding them and using them when you need them.
* The better AI gets, the more your own notes and judgment are worth, because that's context AI doesn't have.
