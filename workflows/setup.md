# Set Me Up

Make this project mine through a short Q&A. Ask these one at a time, and wait for each answer before asking the next. Keep each question to a sentence or two, with a quick example if it helps.

1. **Who are you?** What you do, and what you're working on or learning.
2. **What do you want to post about?** The topics, and how you look at them.
3. **Who do you want to reach, and what do you want from posting?** For example, finding a job, growing a product, or meeting people in your field.
4. **How do you want to sound?** Pasting a few of your past posts, or posts you like from others, works best.
5. **Anything to avoid?** Topics, words, or styles.
6. **Do you have X Premium?**

If my setup message already answers some of these, skip them. If it includes standing instructions, like a hashtag for every post, save them to the writing style.

Then, in one go:

* Rewrite [profile.md](../context/profile.md) from my answers, in my own words, under its headings. Drop the example content. Keep it short and specific.
* If I pasted my own past posts, put them in [x_posts.md](../output/x_posts.md). Remove the example posts there, and delete the example Article in [x_articles/](../output/x_articles/).
* Tell me it's done in a sentence or two, and that I can paste more past posts into `output/x_posts.md` or tell you which X accounts to follow for finding what's hot. Then offer to suggest a first topic.
